(24-006) Little Collins Roadworks

▾ Settings  ›  Retention
The screen today asks for Max Retention 5 and Retention Rate 10 and never says percent of what — they are percentages of two different things, the contract sum and each claim. No dollar amount appears anywhere. This version makes every field state its own denominator and resolve to dollars as you type.

The field that does not exist today is at the bottom: whether retention comes off the invoice. That is VDP-2282. Its absence is how VDP-2251 shipped — production invoices retention-bearing claims gross, and nobody chose that.

1
Percent of what? Max Retention is a percentage of the contract sum. It renders as 5 with no denominator and no dollar equivalent. On this project it is $124,000 — a figure the screen never shows while you are setting it.
2
A different denominator, identically labelled. Retention Rate is a percentage of each claim, not of the contract sum. Two adjacent fields, both reading %, measuring different things.
3
The rate is double the maximum and nothing explains why that is fine. 10% comes off every claim until the 5% cap is reached, then nothing more is held. Read cold, Max 5 / Rate 10 looks like someone typed the fields in the wrong order — and there is no way to tell from this screen that the cap will bind on Claim #4.
4
"Merge Retention" names the control, not the outcome. "Combine Retention Settings for contract and variation into single setting" describes what the toggle does to the form. What the user wants to know is whether variations are retained on the same terms as the contract.
5
The only explanation is inside an info icon. Per VDP-1169 the intent is we still calculate, but let it be edited. "Enable Manual Contract Retention" reads like it turns the calculation off. And there is no visible variation equivalent.
6
A dropdown implying options the labels cannot survive. If Basis is ever set to an amount, "Max Retention %" and "Retention Rate %" are both wrong, and the % is hard-rendered inside the field.
7
Half the form is below the fold with Save floating over the hidden half. VDP-2250 was the non-scrolling version of this; scrolling fixed the failure, not the shape.
8
Nothing here mentions the invoice. The setting deciding whether the client is billed gross or net does not exist — that is VDP-2282, and its absence is how VDP-2251 shipped. Gross invoicing is currently happening by accident rather than by choice.
9
No release terms, no bands, no security. Release at PC and the defects period (VDP-111), the NZS 3910 stepped schedule (VDP-2034) and bank guarantees in lieu all need a home, and this is the screen they belong on.
Nine renames and six additions. No calculation changes to what is already there — the same rate, the same cap, the same merge behaviour. What changes is that each field says what it measures, and the decisions currently made elsewhere (or not at all) get made here.
