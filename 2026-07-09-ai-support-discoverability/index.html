Making the AI Support agent easy to find

Right now the AI agent lives two clicks deep — inside the Need Help? dropdown as one of three cards, plus a small unlabelled chat bubble on the right edge. Below are three ways to surface it, mocked on the live Projects toolbar, ordered from lightest to boldest change.

1

Dedicated “Ask AI” button in the toolbar

Lift the agent out of the dropdown and give it its own persistent, labelled button next to Need Help?. Distinct AI colour and a “live” dot signal it’s an instant, always-on assistant — not another menu.

2

Labelled floating “Ask AI” pill upgrade the bubble

Keep the persistent corner presence, but replace the tiny unlabelled bubble with a clear pill so people know what it is. Follows the pattern users expect from Intercom / Zendesk-style assistants.

Promote AI to the top of the “Need Help?” panel

Smallest change of all — keep the dropdown, but make the AI agent the hero card at the top (full-width, coloured, badged) instead of the last of three equal tiles.
