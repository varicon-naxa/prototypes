What's new in this mock-up

  • Added: an All Bills / My Bills toggle above the tabs — the same control already on the Purchase Order screen ("All Purchase Orders / My Purchase Orders").
  • My Bills filters to bills where the logged-in user (here, SD — Sanil, shown as the highlighted avatar) is an assigned approver, surfacing the ones Pending you first.
  • My Bills defaults to bills at your step — i.e. earlier approvers have already approved and it's now awaiting you (e.g. as 2nd approver, Mitch has approved — shown green). Keeps the list to "what I can action right now".
  • Force / approve-ahead (item A3): the checkbox "Also show bills pending an earlier approver" reveals bills where you're in the chain but an earlier approver hasn't approved yet — letting you approve ahead (force approve) with accountability on you. Today only the 3rd approver can do this; this lets the 2nd (any approver) do it too.
  • Why: today approvers scan the full Awaiting Approval (15) and Partially Approved (36) lists to find their own — so bills get missed (41 overdue, $82,387.21). And when an earlier approver is away, the bill stalls. This fixes both.
