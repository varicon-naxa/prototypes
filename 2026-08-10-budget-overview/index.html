What type of variation is this?

A variation adds to the contract sum and carries its own budget. Once approved it is tracked in the overview exactly like a BOQ line — POs commit it, dockets track it, paid bills make it actual.

Priced Variation
Agreed pricing based on a lump sum quote or a build-up from your schedule of rates.
Dayworks Variation
Tracked on a time and cost basis. Pull in approved daywork dockets already booked on site.
Cost Plus Variation
Actual cost plus an agreed margin percentage.

How would you like to price it?

This sets how the contract value is built up. The budget cost is set separately in the next step.

Lump Sum
Enter agreed line items with quantities and rates.
Build Up Using Rates
Price from the schedule of rates — labour, plant and materials.

Add daywork dockets to this variation

Instruct against the allowance
The work is covered by the provisional allowance already in the contract. The activated amount moves into the live contract and budget at the agreed value.
Activate as a variation
The instructed scope exceeds the allowance or differs from it. Creates a submitted variation for the difference, which needs client approval before it counts.
