Labour & Time
One place for who was on site, how long they worked, and what they worked on.
Timecard and Labour are now one step. The hours you enter here flow straight to the timesheet for payroll and to labour cost against the work — no re-entering the same person twice.
Labour is added on a docket. The docket is the source document, and the order behind it sets the rate — so nobody types a rate into the diary.
Plant & Equipment
Every machine on the project, and what it did today — worked, stood down, or never turned up.
An idle machine still costs, so the ones that did nothing are listed too. Tap a status to stand a machine down — the register prices it, the diary records it.
Materials
Each line is an order — a purchase order, or a bill where nothing was ordered first. Tap one to see the dockets behind it. Everything below is as at the day shown.
Miscellaneous
Anything else billable to the job — subcontract claims, disposal, permits, sundries. Same rule: an order is a PO, or a bill where nothing was ordered.
Site dockets
Paper dockets captured on site. Each one is the source document for an entry — saving it creates the matching line in Labour, Plant, Materials or Misc and links back here. A docket lands against an order, and the order is what carries the rate.

Site Diary — mobile, matched to the desktop

The mobile diary rebuilt against the budget-connected prototype's Site Diary tab, so the field app and the office screen describe the same day the same way.

  • Timecard is gone — hours are entered once and feed payroll and cost.
  • Plant is the day's roster, not just machines with cost. Stand-down is a short flow: why, how much of the day, what it is charged to.
  • Materials and Misc are orders, snapshotted at the day shown. Step the date and watch delivered move.
  • Supervisor view masks every rate and cost through one formatter.
  • Sign and submit lives in the footer, on every section.
  • Everything is added on a docket — labour included — and every docket links to an order. There is no rate field: the order carries the rate, so one thing has one price. A docket with no order is still captured, flagged unmatched, and matched later.

Static HTML — no build step. See notes.md.
