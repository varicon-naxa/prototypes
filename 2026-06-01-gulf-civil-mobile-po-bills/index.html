What's new in this mock-up

  • Bottom nav — Purchase Orders & Bills added (marked NEW). Today the admin/owner bottom nav is Projects · Forms · Leave · Gallery · Files — neither POs nor Bills are there.
  • Purchase Orders — across ALL projects. Today POs on mobile are reachable only inside a single project (e.g. Caravonica → Purchase Order). Screen 2 shows an all-projects view with an "Awaiting you" filter.
  • Bills — now on mobile at all. Bills approval doesn't exist on mobile today. Screens 3–4 add an all-projects bills list and a basic see-&-approve detail that mirrors the desktop approver flow (approver chain Mitch → Sanil → Ramesh, view invoice, line items, Approve / Reject / Approve & next).
  • Lock-screen push notifications (item A4): Screen 0 — a new bill or PO awaiting approval (and comments on bills they approve) pushes to the lock screen; tap straight into the approval. So nothing waits on someone manually opening the app.
  • Owner-first: open the app → banner shows "6 bills & 2 POs awaiting your approval" → tap → approve from the phone. Built for Romesh, who is mobile-first and often overseas.
  • Approval step + force-approve (item A3): "My Bills" defaults to Awaiting me — bills at your step (earlier approver already done, shown green). The + Pending earlier chip shows bills where an earlier approver hasn't approved yet; Screen 5 lets you approve ahead / force approve with accountability on you. Today only the 3rd approver can do this — this lets the 2nd (any approver) do it too.
  • Scope: basic functionality — see + approve/reject. Not full bill editing on mobile.
