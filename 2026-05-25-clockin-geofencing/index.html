Why block instead of warn: Geofencing exists to ensure time is recorded against real on-site presence — silently allowing off-site clock-ins defeats the purpose. The override path keeps legitimate exceptions (depot pickups, travel time, GPS error) unblocked while leaving an audit trail for the PM. Distance, GPS accuracy, and timestamp are auto-attached so the manager doesn't have to ask.
