Prototype — Claim → Xero invoice → paid sync

  • Create Invoice in Xero (bottom bar) is available once the claim is approved. The invoice is pre-filled from the claim: reference = claim # + project, claim period carried onto the invoice, amount = claim total (+ GST), and the Progress Claim PDF is attached.
  • After creation the claim shows the live Xero status (invoice #, due date, awaiting payment).
  • When the client pays in Xero, a webhook syncs the payment back — the claim flips to PAID with the payment date, amount and reference. No manual reconciliation in Varicon.
