What's new on the PDF
  1. Retention section. Prints only when the project uses stepped retention. States the schedule as a sentence, then shows each band. Flat-rate projects print the summary as they do today.
  2. Certified in band to date. Bands are marginal. A claim that crosses a step is split across both rates (try Claim #5). The rate of the top band is not applied to the whole value.
  3. Held this claim = held to date − held before. It is worked out from the cumulative position, never from this claim's value alone. That way a claim that crosses a step comes out right, and so does a claim that reduces value certified.
  4. Summary rows unchanged. Retention Withheld carries the same figures as the Retention table's total row, with a pointer down to it. When Complete is the schedule applied to the revised contract value.
  5. Cap. The $200,000 maximum applies after the bands and gets its own row once it binds. It doesn't bind on this contract.
