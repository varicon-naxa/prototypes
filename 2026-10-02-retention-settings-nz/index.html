What's new here

Deduct Retention from Invoice (VAR-22037): every tenant, AU and NZ. On by default, which is today's behaviour. Turn it off on a project to invoice the full claim value. NZ customers usually do this so the principal can hold the retention in trust.

Stepped by value (VAR-20740): NZ tenants only, behind the NZ feature flag. Prefilled with the NZS 3910 default, editable per project. Choosing it merges contract and variation retention into one schedule. Australian tenants see the modal exactly as today plus the deduct toggle.

The NZS 3910 bands and $200,000 cap still need confirming against the published standard before build.
