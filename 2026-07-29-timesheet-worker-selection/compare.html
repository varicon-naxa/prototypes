Selecting workers doesn't look like selecting anything

On the Add Timesheet employee picker, ticking a worker gives almost no feedback, and the only way to commit the selection is a small arrow top-left or a small tick top-right. Neither reads as "done". This is a redesign of that one sheet.

What changes, and why

Five numbered problems, matched to the pins on both phones.

1
Now

A back arrow sits top-left. It's the same arrow that means "discard" everywhere else in the app, so nobody trusts it — does leaving keep my ticks or throw them away?

Proposed

Replace it with the word Cancel. A word can only mean one thing. Discarding is now explicit, and the arrow stops competing with the confirm action.

2
Now

The only way to commit is a small gold tick in the top-right corner — the far corner of the screen, no label, no fill, easy to miss entirely on a phone held one-handed.

Proposed

A full-width primary button pinned to the bottom that says "Add 2 workers". It states the count and the outcome, sits under the thumb, and is impossible to miss.

3
Now

A ticked row looks identical to an unticked one apart from a 21px checkbox at the far right edge. Scanning a crew of twenty, you cannot see who you've picked.

Proposed

The whole row changes: warm tint, orange left rail, ringed avatar with a tick badge, and the subtitle flips to "Added". Selection is readable at a glance, from across the row.

4
Now

Rows read "Sandy Groundes (Sandy Groundes)" — the name printed twice — and every row under the Assigned tab is subtitled "Unassigned", which contradicts the tab it sits under.

Proposed

Name once. Subtitle carries something useful — the worker's trade or crew. Tabs renamed On this project / Everyone else, which is what the split actually means.

5
Now

The "Selected Employees" tray is docked at the very bottom, so the keyboard covers it the moment you tap search. The count is a pale badge floated off to the right.

Proposed

The tray sits directly above the button and both stay above the keyboard. The count moves into the button label — one number, in the place you're already looking.

Build notes

Enough detail to scope it without another round trip.

  • ▸The whole row is the tap target, not just the checkbox. Minimum 48dp row height.
  • ▸Footer pins with safe-area-inset-bottom and resizes with the keyboard, so the button is never occluded.
  • ▸Button label is a plural-aware string: Add 1 worker / Add 2 workers. Disabled at zero, reading Select workers to add — a disabled button with no explanation is its own bug.
  • ▸Selected tint #FFF6EC, left rail #F37A21 at 3px. Both already in the palette — no new tokens.
  • ▸Chips in the tray scroll horizontally and each one removes its own worker. Tray hides entirely at zero selected.
  • ▸Select all toggles to Clear all once everything visible is picked, and respects the active filter.
  • ▸Cancel with unsaved ticks should confirm before discarding. Nothing else in this sheet needs a dialog.

One thing I'd look at separately. Once you're back on Add Timesheet, there's a second selection layer — a "Selected Employees / Select Employees to bulk edit" checkbox that flips the header to 2 SELECTED with a bin icon.

So the same two workers get selected twice, in two different visual languages, one screen apart. Worth a look, but it's a separate change from this one and shouldn't hold this up.

Mockup only — not production code. Behaviour and copy are the proposal; spacing is indicative. Device frames render the app in its own light theme in both page themes, since the app doesn't have a dark mode.
