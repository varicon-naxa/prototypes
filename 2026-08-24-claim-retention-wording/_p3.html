Seven things this screen cannot tell you

  1. “Released Retention” is the heading over a row called Withheld. The section covers both directions but is named after one of them.
  2. “Total Previous Held” and “Retention Held” sit side by side in a table called This Claim. Nothing says one is history and the other is this claim.
  3. “When Complete” is a column header that reads like a status. It means the amount that falls due at practical completion.
  4. “Previous Claim” is singular. Is that the last claim, or every claim before this one?
  5. A release of $4,782.52 is shown against $0.00 withheld, with no link to the claim it came from. The figure cannot be traced or checked.
  6. Max Retention and Retention Rate both show -. A dash reads as zero, as loading, and as not-applicable. It is actually never set up, and nothing invites you to set it up.
  7. The only four numbers that answer what do we invoice are unlabelled, right-aligned in the bottom corner, and cut off the edge of the screen. Revised Total — revised from what?
