What changed, and why

No calculation changes. Every figure on the live screen is still here. What changes is that each label now says which money, whose money and when — and the four numbers that decide the invoice have moved out of the bottom-right corner and become the loudest thing on the page.

One direction, top to bottom

The terms, then the running balance, then this claim’s movement, then the invoice. Today the screen reads left-to-right across two unrelated tables and finishes in a corner.

Every label names its period

“Before this claim”, “on this claim”, “after this claim”. Never a bare “Total” or “Previous” that could mean either.

A dash is not a value

Unconfigured retention says so in words and offers the setup, instead of printing - in three columns.

Every amount is traceable

The balance opens up into the claims that built it. A release of $4,782.52 against $0.00 withheld should be one click from its own explanation.
