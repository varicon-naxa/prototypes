Edit budget lines

Correct anything that came through wrong in the upload — descriptions, quantities, rates, budgets, and which cost centre or zone a line belongs to. Variations VO and instructed provisional sums PS are here too: their budget is editable, their contract value is not, because that is set by the client assessment and the instruction. Lines that already carry cost can be edited but not deleted, because the cost would have nowhere to go.

Instruct at the contract rate
The quantity changes, the rate does not. Value is quantity x the rate already in the contract, and it moves straight into the live contract and budget.
Convert to a variation
