Site Diary — mobile

Two changes to the diary on mobile.

1. Labour and Tasks hidden by default

Neither appears in the hub list or the side rail out of the box. Tap ⋯ → Manage sections on the hub to switch them back on per project.

2. Submit from any section

Every section screen carries the same sticky footer as the hub: a visited-progress line and a Sign and submit button. No more backing out to the previous screen to submit. The footer points at the next unvisited section, and the button turns green once every visible section has been opened.

Try it

  • Note the hub list — no Labour, no Tasks.
  • Open Diary, then use the rail to move through sections; watch the footer count.
  • Hit Sign and submit from inside a section.
  • ⋯ → Manage sections → turn Labour on and see it appear in both places.
