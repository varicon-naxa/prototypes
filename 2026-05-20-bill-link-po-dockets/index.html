Bill: Link PO & Dockets — Redesign

VAR-20317 · Bills detail / edit page · 20 May 2026

Problems

1. The "Link PO and Dockets" button is easy to skip — it's a small, cramped button in the summary row, not built for touch. The redesign makes it a clear primary step with big fat tap targets and a simple sequential flow.

2. When a user selects a PO that isn't approved, the system shows a bare error with no next step. The error state prototype adds an inline panel with the PO name, a plain-language explanation, and two clear actions: Approve now or Choose another PO.

Link PO & Dockets — Happy Path
👆

Fat Buttons — Inline

Two large full-width buttons sit below the summary. Tap one → a simple list of fat rows drops in below. One tap selects. No search, no panels.

Recommended
→

Sequential Steps

PO first, then dockets. Each step is a fullscreen-style card with 2–3 huge choices. One screen, one decision at a time. Works great on tablet.

Alternative
Error States
⚠️

PO Not Approved

User selects an unapproved PO. An inline panel appears below that row with the PO number, a clear explanation, and two fat CTAs: Approve now or Choose another PO.
